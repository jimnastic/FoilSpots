Sydney Wingfoil — Spots by Wind Direction

Sydney spots ranked by preference for each wind direction.

N North

  • 1
    Kurnell~30 min
    Southern Botany Bay shore — N is directly onshore. Flat, less gusty. Only confirmed N wind option from the northern harbour suburbs.
⚠️ N is a difficult direction from the harbour/northern beaches. No good close alternative — Kurnell is a long drive from most of Sydney's north.

NE North-East

  • 1
    Balmoral Beach5 min up to ~20 kn
    Side-shore, flat harbour. Classic summer seabreeze spot. Chop builds above 20 kn.
  • 2
    Middle Creek (Narrabeen Lagoon)12 min 20+ kn
    Flat water regardless of strength — enclosed lagoon stays calm when Balmoral chops up.

E East

  • 1
    Middle Creek (Narrabeen Lagoon)12 min ideal
    E blows directly across the lagoon toward the west shore. Flat, contained, no swell.

SE South-East

  • 1
    Clontarf5 min light–mod SE
    Convenient Middle Harbour option. Works in SE/SSE. Flat, sheltered. Use for quick sessions or lighter winds.
  • 2
    Kyeemagh (Botany Bay)25–30 min solid SE
    SE is Kyeemagh's ideal direction. Better wind quality, more consistent, bigger water. Worth the drive for a proper session.
  • 3
    Bilarong (Narrabeen Lagoon)12 min
    Flat-water fallback. SE is at the edge of Bilarong's range but workable.
⚠️ Check rainfall 48 hrs before Kyeemagh — Cook's River runoff causes E. coli spikes.

S South

  • 1
    Kyeemagh (Botany Bay)25–30 min moderate S only
    Works in moderate S (12–18 kn). Chop builds above 18 kn — flat-ish near the beach in lee of Cook's River mouth.
  • 2
    Bilarong (Narrabeen Lagoon)12 min
    Closer flat-water option. S is within Bilarong's range (north shore facing SSW).
  • 3
    Kurnell30 min 20+ kn buster
    When S becomes a strong southerly buster (20+ kn), switch to Kurnell — windward shore stays flat where Kyeemagh gets rough.
⚠️ Check rainfall 48 hrs before Kyeemagh.

SW South-West

  • 1
    Bilarong (Narrabeen Lagoon)12 min default
    SW is Bilarong's ideal direction. North shore faces SSW — wind blows cleanly across the lagoon. Closer and more contained than alternatives.
  • 2
    Rose Bay (Sydney Harbour)~22 min 18+ kn only
    Open harbour can outperform sheltered lagoon when SW is strong and clean. Not worth the drive for marginal days.

W West

  • 1
    Bilarong (Narrabeen Lagoon)12 min marginal
    W is at the edge of Bilarong's range. Worth trying if you don't want the extra drive.
  • 2
    Pittwater (Church Point / Sandy Point)~35 min cleaner wind
    Pittwater gives cleaner W wind than the lagoon. Sandy Point suits SW/W; Church Point better for NW/W. Both gusty — expect lulls and surges.

NW North-West

  • 1
    Pittwater (Church Point)~35 min ideal
    Western Pittwater shores face into NW wind — cleaner and more consistent than the lagoon which gets rotor off the ridge. Good flat-water fetch.
⚠️ Neither Narrabeen Lagoon site is well-positioned for NW. Pittwater is the call.
